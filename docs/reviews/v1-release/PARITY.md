# V1 release decision and feature parity

**HOLD mobile v1.** Replacing the prototype today would remove core learning paths. Main has a working lesson, practice and offline-sync foundation. Its API contract contains health, readiness, identity, catalog, lesson and sync operations. It has no controlled-exam, tutor, social or notification endpoint. The approved curriculum inventory, migration reconciliation and release-device evidence are also missing. Keep the existing service available while qualifying v1. Chess, Découvre ta passion and new in-app purchases are outside this release decision. Remaining access that an existing learner already earned or purchased is in scope.

Reviewed native/backend main `1094eb0822759d7bc28f3162285077f30ad38969` and prototype source `3110f7971aad9abbdeb3e2965afd565f5b74b520`. The owner reports 61 current students. No student accounts were queried. The prototype evidence below demonstrates code paths, not the deployed revision, production inventory, retention of every historical result, or live service quality. Use an owner-supplied redacted demonstration and aggregate inventory to close those gaps. Native evidence distinguishes main, previously run test suites, and work on other branches. ## Release risk register

| Priority | Harm and trigger | Evidence needed to close | Accountable role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | A returning learner receives another learner’s identity, loses history, or loses paid access after sign-in. | Idempotent dual-run reconciliation, zero silent merges/overwrites, verified recovery exceptions and a rollback mapping. | Identity owner and independent data reviewer |
| Critical | Private messages, attachments, notifications or academic records appear on a shared phone after account switch or removal. | All denied-identity cases pass across API, cache, realtime and delivery. Inspect both signed binaries. | Security and mobile leads |
| Critical | Community or Jude exposes minors to unsafe interaction with no effective intervention. | Safety benchmark by locale, staffed escalation, block/report tests, provider withdrawal and tested kill switches. | Safeguarding, moderation and AI leads |
| High | Catalog labels imply complete learning pathways while lessons, assets or tracks are missing or withdrawn. | Signed expected inventory, zero unexplained differences, source rights and bilingual review. | Curriculum and rights owners |
| High | Leaked controlled-exam answers, local score authority or replay corrupts results and history. | Independent expected scores, sealed-question tests, deadline/resume/idempotency results and historical reconciliation. | Assessment and backend leads |
| High | A disconnected learner loses pending attempts after replacement, deletion, sign-out, upgrade or rollback. | Durable queue fault tests with exact before/after per-account records and device evidence. | Sync and mobile leads |
| High | Hosted restore, budget enforcement, required alert delivery or store/privacy qualification fails after rollout. | Operational drills and provider receipts, approved budgets, monitored age-appropriate settings and signed artifact evidence. | Release operator and privacy lead |

## Dependencies and recommended order

1. Freeze the expected legacy curriculum and account/history mapping, exam taxonomy, v1 notification scope, consent and moderation rules. Keep the legacy service usable. The reported 61 is a reconciliation checkpoint. Reconfirm the aggregate at cutover.
2. Land independently reviewed staging fixes on their intended parents, close #47, then rehearse hosted backup/restore, audit and rollback. Reconcile migration order across staff #4/#17, identity #5 and entitlement #10 before a shared integration candidate. Rebuild a disposable database from blank and rehearse upgrade from the current schema.
3. Integrate identity mapping, access grants, reviewed bilingual content publication, withdrawal and offline sync. Preserve legacy keys and pending learning operations. Main already includes #3/#25/#26. The open #22 label alone gives no reason to re-merge it.
4. Build controlled exams on authoritative identity and reviewed revisions. Qualify Jude using #28/#43 and the separately owned #35 evaluation work. Build social authorization/moderation before activating social notifications. Merge the reviewed notification base and corrections in dependency order. Track cross-branch migration IDs and regenerate clients at the combined SHA.
5. On a pinned release candidate, run the [acceptance gates](ACCEPTANCE.md) and [synthetic vectors](fixtures.json), obtain named human sign-offs, then rehearse a limited opt-in migration with rollback before any wider cutover. Preserve an existing student's service until their continuity is verified. No release, merge or deployment is authorized by this report.
