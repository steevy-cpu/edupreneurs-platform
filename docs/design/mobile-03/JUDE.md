# Jude: future contextual presence

Jude is not connected to the student app. This is a design specification, not a feature or a promise of live assistance. Obtain and verify the owner's official character artwork and usage rules before implementation; no character asset has been verified for reuse in this pass.

- Home: an optional, compact welcome alongside the learning action, never above an urgent offline or account notice.
- Lesson: a contextual help entry adjacent to relevant content, only after real assistance is available and its availability is clear.
- Practice: a hint only when an approved hint exists; distinguish authored content from a live service.
- Progress and empty states: brief encouragement grounded in actual activity, without invented achievements or statistics.

Use a static, optimized, appropriately sized approved asset; reserve space to avoid layout shifts and omit decoration when space or text scale demands it. A text-only presentation must stand alone. Provide meaningful names for functional controls and hide purely decorative images from screen readers. Avoid autoplay, persistent overlays, unnecessary requests and motion. French and Haitian Creole wording needs educator review. Never present fake AI responses, an enabled chat, or an interactive control without a working destination.
