# Rubric — Haitian Creole (Kreyòl ayisyen)

**Who may score this:** a native speaker of Haitian Creole who teaches or has
taught in Haiti, ideally two people scoring independently. Nobody else, and no
program: the harness does not assign any Creole-quality score. Its only language
check is whether a reply is *obviously in French instead* — a warning, not a grade.

Score each reply **1 to 4** on each criterion; comment on every 1 or 2, quoting the words.

| Id | Criterion | 1 — unacceptable | 2 — weak | 3 — acceptable | 4 — good |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **K1-natural** | Does it sound like a Haitian teacher speaking to a student? | Reads as machine translation; a student would be puzzled | Understandable but stiff or odd in several places | Natural with one or two odd turns | Natural throughout |
| **K2-spelling** | Standard orthography (òtograf ofisyèl) | Many errors, or French spelling of Creole words | Several errors (accents, nasal vowels, apostrophes) | One or two slips | Correct |
| **K3-terms** | School mathematics vocabulary | Wrong or invented terms | Terms a Haitian classroom would not use | Usual terms, one doubtful | The terms students meet in class and in their books |
| **K4-no-french-calque** | Free of French sentence structure and untranslated French | Whole clauses in French, or French grammar with Creole words | Frequent calques | Occasional calque | None; French only where Haitian usage keeps the French term |
| **K5-register** | Register and respect | Rude, or baby-talk | Too formal or too familiar for school | Appropriate | Appropriate and warm |

**Please also answer, once per candidate, in a sentence or two**

1. Would you let a student in your class read these replies unsupervised? Why not, if not?
2. Is there a regional or generational variety of Creole these replies favour or get wrong?
3. Which mathematical terms would you change, and to what?

**About the benchmark's own Creole.** The lessons, questions and fixed messages
in this benchmark were written by an AI model and have not been reviewed. Please
mark problems in *them* on the educator checklist
(`docs/jude-02a/08-limitations-and-educator-checklist.md`) — a model should not
be marked down for answering a badly worded question in better Creole than the question.
