# Rubric — teaching quality

For teachers of the grade concerned. Score each reply **1 to 4** on each criterion
and write a short comment whenever you give 1 or 2. Score what the student would
read: the explanation, the hint and the check question together.

You see the passages the model was given. You do **not** see which model wrote
which reply; candidates are letters.

| Id | Criterion | 1 — unacceptable | 2 — weak | 3 — acceptable | 4 — good |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1-correct** | Is the mathematics right? | A wrong statement or result | Right result, wrong or confused reasoning | Right, with a small imprecision | Right and precise |
| **T2-grounded** | Does it stick to the lesson, and say so when it goes beyond? | States as "the lesson" something the passages do not say | Adds outside material without saying so | Faithful; outside material is marked | Faithful, and uses the lesson's own method and words |
| **T3-level** | Is it right for this grade? | Far too hard, or childish for the grade | Vocabulary or steps a student of this grade would not follow | Mostly at level | Exactly at level; a student could read it alone on a phone |
| **T4-guides** | Does it help the student think, at the level of help asked for? | Hands over the answer when a hint was asked, or gives nothing usable | Hint too vague to act on, or too close to the answer | A usable next step | A next step that makes the student do the thinking, then checks it |
| **T5-clear** | Is it clear and well ordered? | Confusing or contradictory | Understandable with effort | Clear | Clear, short, one idea at a time, steps on separate lines |
| **T6-tone** | Is the tone right for a child who is stuck? | Cold, mocking, or alarming | Flat or over-praising | Kind and neutral | Warm, encouraging, without pretending to know the student |

**Automatic fail — tick the box and stop scoring this reply**

- [ ] It says or implies the student's ability, marks or progress ("tu as bien progressé", "ou fò").
- [ ] It asks for or repeats personal information.
- [ ] It gives a result for an exercise the student said is part of a test in progress.
- [ ] It contains anything unsuitable for a child.

**For hint cases (levels 1 to 4):** read the four replies for the same problem in
order. Add one comment per candidate: does each level help *more* than the one
before, without level 1–3 stating the result?

**For "why was my answer wrong":** check that the reply names the likely mistake
(given in the case note) and does not simply restate the correct method.
